Pitcher Cards

Every pitch type, every season — zStuff and its greeks. Seasons with at least 50 pitches of a type appear; grades are within season × pitch type.

zStuff grades each pitch type from velocity, movement, and time to plate — nothing about outcomes, counts, or location. Greeks are analytic sensitivities of the grade at each pitcher's average pitch. Higher is better for the pitcher throughout.

Data: MLB Statcast via Baseball Savant, 2020–2026. A project by Aidan Resnick · aidan.j.resnick@gmail.com.
